# Public recruitment-market data

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Job Openings: Total Nonfarm [JTSJOL], retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

- Series page: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/JTSJOL
- Download: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=JTSJOL
- Download date: 2026-10-28
- Units: thousands of job openings, seasonally adjusted, monthly, United States total nonfarm.
- Series tagged Public Domain: Citation Requested by FRED.
- Raw snapshot SHA-256: B2103436EB1F5CB9FE30AC9B65EB3D895F69758DF1C52476778E50AC55027C53

This measures unfilled positions on the last business day of the month. It is not the number of applicants, offers or hires. It is not specific to China, Didi, AI occupations, or any employer. A decline in openings can reflect either filled or withdrawn positions; the series alone cannot distinguish the causes.

Data are revised and released with a lag. The supplied CSV is a latest-revised download, not an ALFRED historical vintage. The analysis is retrospective chronological holdout evaluation, not a claim of trading-style real-time backtesting.
